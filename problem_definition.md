# 1. Problem Definition

## Problem
Pet owners often struggle to keep track of their pets’ vaccination schedules, medical history, medications, and other important health activities. Important records may be scattered across documents or difficult to access when needed, leading to missed vaccinations, delayed medical care, and poor tracking of a pet’s overall health.

There is a need for a centralized system that helps pet owners securely maintain their pets’ health information, record vaccination and medical history, and receive timely reminders for upcoming or overdue vaccinations and other scheduled health activities.

## Solution

> **PawPet**  
> A system that allows pet owners to maintain health information for their pets, record vaccination and medical history, and receive reminders for upcoming or overdue vaccinations and other scheduled health activities.

---

## Objectives
- **Centralized Health Repository:** Provide a single secure place to log and review pet medical histories.
- **Automated Reminder System:** Ensure pet owners never miss critical vaccination schedules, checkups, or medication doses.
- **Accessibility & Simplicity:** Offer a user-friendly interface accessible across devices for quick updates during vet visits.

## Scope
- **In-Scope:** Pet profile creation, medical record logging, vaccination schedule tracking, reminder notifications (email/push), history exports.
- **Out-of-Scope (Initial Phase):** Direct in-app veterinary video consultations, pet insurance claim processing, ecommerce store integration.
